This file switches Mokuro Bunko to portable mode.

While it is here, run.bat and doctor.bat keep everything (config, database,
library, logs, OCR models) in the data\ folder next to them, and nothing is
written to AppData or the registry.

Delete it to use the normal locations instead
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\mokuro-bunko, where mokuro-bunko 0.5 kept its library).
scripts\install.ps1 deletes it for you.
