        Reference: quality and hardware of each setup

        How the setups compare on quality, which no benchmark can measure. For the speed of any of them on your machine and your pages, use Benchmark & tune on that generation's row above.

        SetupQualityMinimum hardware
        hayai-nova + ppocr-mangaThe default. Accurate on dialogue, reads covers and colored lettering. Its bf16 tested more accurate than fp32; fp16 is the one that loses.any CPU, 2 GB RAM; on a card, bf16 needs about 0.9 GB of GPU memory
        paddle-manga + ppocr-mangaBest on Japanese display text and sound effects. Most accurate in fp32, and much faster in bf16 on cards whose hardware runs bf16 natively.6 GB RAM on the CPU, where it is slow (about 40 s a page on 16 threads); on a card, fp32 takes about 4.1 GB of GPU memory, bf16 about 2.2 GB
        ppocr-mangaLine-level: rotated text, furigana removed; reads novels. Sets its own precision.any CPU, 1 GB RAM (runs on the CPU only)

        Detector: ppocr-manga, which finds the text lines every engine reads.

        Precision is one mode per generation, for every machine. Auto: accuracy (the default) picks what tested most accurate for each engine; Auto: balanced gives up a little accuracy for a lot of speed; Auto: speed takes the fastest format each card runs well. For balanced and speed, each machine benchmarks the generation automatically before its first volume, and again when the mode changes, and keeps the fastest format its card supports. fp32, bf16 or fp16 only force that format, and a machine whose card cannot run it is skipped: it is never given that generation's volumes.
